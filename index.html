Who we are

About the department

The Department of Computing provides an environment where students develop theoretical knowledge and practical skills in modern computing. Learning combines classroom instruction, laboratory practice, independent study and collaborative projects.

Our departmental website is a central information point for students seeking programme details, academic activities, lecture schedules and registration guidance. Official dates, venues and notices should always be confirmed through the university’s authorised channels.
